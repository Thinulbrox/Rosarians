About Us

Rosarians is a dynamic musical group that brings together talented musicians with diverse backgrounds and influences. Founded in 2009, we've been crafting unforgettable musical experiences for audiences across the country.

Our unique sound blends elements of rock, indie, and alternative music, creating a signature style that's both familiar and refreshingly original. Whether we're performing at intimate venues or large festivals, we bring the same passion and energy to every show.

With over 200 live performances under our belt and over 15 years of excellence, the Rosarians have built a reputation for electrifying shows that leave audiences wanting more. Our commitment to musical excellence and authentic connection with our fans has made us one of the most sought-after bands in the region.
